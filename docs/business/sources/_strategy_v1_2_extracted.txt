Executive Summary
MentionBee operates in a market that is real, fast-growing and already heavily financed. That is both the opportunity and the warning. Profound and Peec have demonstrated that brands will pay to understand how they appear in AI answers. They have also raised enough capital to make a head-on feature race unattractive for a bootstrapped entrant.
Core strategic conclusion: MentionBee should not try to become a smaller Peec or Profound, and it does not need a globally unique feature to deserve a place in the market. The near-term job is simpler: become an unusually useful, trusted and memorable way for SMEs to understand why AI systems recommend competitors and what to do about it. Differentiation matters; absolute uniqueness does not.
The current paid report is good enough to sell now: it combines competitor comparison, source analysis, technical and content checks, and prioritized recommendations. The next bottleneck is not report quality. It is distribution, trust, proof and sales. Therefore the report enters a 90-day Product Freeze: no major redesign, scoring rewrite, dashboard build or reliability architecture unless customer evidence shows that a specific product change materially affects purchase, implementation or retention.
The near-term revenue problem is solved by proving demand and building a repeatable acquisition motion, not by adding features. MentionBee must show that people outside the founder's network will pay, that the report causes useful action, and that at least one acquisition path can be repeated without consuming the founder's week.
The five decisions this paper recommends
Reposition the paid product around diagnosis and decisions, not around an AI Visibility Score.
Make external proof - starting with MEMS - the primary trust mechanism on the website.
Keep the €190 one-off Deep-Dive as the wedge product; do not build a broad SaaS dashboard now.
Build traffic through research, comparison pages, high-intent problem pages and credible distribution - not through generic GEO content volume.
Run a 90-day revenue program whose success criterion is two paying new customers and evidence of one repeatable acquisition path.
What would make the thesis fail
The strategy is falsifiable. MentionBee should reconsider the product if, after a focused 90-day program, qualified prospects understand the problem but still will not pay €190; if customers repeatedly say the report is interesting but does not change action; or if acquisition requires so much manual education that the economics cannot work at the current price.

1. Market Thesis: Why This Category Exists
The underlying behavior change is no longer hypothetical: consumers and business buyers increasingly use conversational systems to discover, compare and shortlist products. At the same time, AI answers often resolve information needs without sending a click to the source. This means the commercial value of being represented correctly can grow even when referral traffic remains smaller than traditional search.
That distinction matters for MentionBee. If the company promises 'more website traffic from ChatGPT', it makes a claim the market evidence does not reliably support. If it promises 'understand and improve whether your brand is considered and recommended inside AI-mediated discovery', the proposition is broader and more defensible.

Recent academic work reinforces the caution around traffic claims. A 2026 clickstream study found outbound clicks in only a small minority of ChatGPT information-seeking sessions, while a separate field study found that raw ChatGPT referral growth can substantially overstate the causal effect of optimization because the platform itself is growing quickly. The commercial opportunity is real, but the measurement discipline must be stronger than the hype.
Market verdict: attractive enough to enter, but too competitive to enter generically. MentionBee needs a sharp wedge, evidence and distribution - not a feature checklist.

8. Traffic Reverse Engineering
Important limitation: this is public-footprint reverse engineering, not access to competitor GA4 or server logs. Exact traffic volumes and channel shares cannot be known from public pages alone. What can be observed reliably is the acquisition architecture competitors are building.
Peec: research as distribution
Peec's public blog is not a generic SEO blog. It repeatedly publishes proprietary-data studies: 30 million sources, one million citations, 232,000 citations, 65M+ citations across countries, query-fanout analyses and case studies. This creates four loops at once: search demand, citations/backlinks, social discussion and AI-source eligibility.
Peec also publishes practical guides and case studies, but the distinctive traffic asset is data. The research itself becomes a reason for journalists, SEOs, LinkedIn users and AI systems to reference Peec.
Profound: category ownership + comparison capture
Profound combines category education with aggressive comparison content: reviews of Peec, Writesonic, AthenaHQ, SE Visible, Ahrefs Brand Radar and others, plus 'best AI visibility provider' pages. This captures bottom-of-funnel searches from buyers who already know the category. Profound also uses funding announcements, enterprise customer stories and thought leadership to build authority.
Visibisy: simplicity and product-led entry
Visibisy's public proposition is much narrower: enter the company and ideal customer, generate realistic prompts, track a weekly score across four LLMs. Its acquisition advantage is likely low-friction product-led discovery rather than a visible research/content moat.

10. Distribution: PR, LinkedIn, Reddit and Medium
PR should be earned with data, not with product announcements. A headline such as '72% of 100 Swiss industrial SMEs were never recommended across 20 purchase-intent AI queries' has a chance of being reported; 'MentionBee launches AI Visibility Report' does not.
The research design must be transparent enough to survive scrutiny: sample definition, prompt set, models, dates, repeated runs and limitations.
LinkedIn
Use founder-led learning rather than promotional posts. A useful cadence is one strong observation or experiment per week: a before/after result, a surprising source pattern, a failed hypothesis, or a short teardown of an anonymized company. The goal is to make MentionBee synonymous with evidence, not with GEO hot takes.
Reddit
Reddit can influence both human discovery and AI citations, but covert promotion is strategically dangerous. Participate only where there is genuine expertise to contribute. Do not manufacture mentions, sock-puppet discussions or spam comparison threads. The durable asset is reputation.
Medium and external publishing
Use Medium or similar platforms for research summaries and essays that can reach audiences outside the owned domain, but keep canonical, evidence-rich versions on MentionBee where possible. External articles should create a path back to a unique dataset, tool or report rather than duplicate generic advice.
Distribution rule: Every content asset needs a distribution plan before it is produced: who specifically will care, where they already gather, and what makes the asset worth sharing?
11. Trust Architecture
Trust is the immediate commercial bottleneck. MentionBee is asking a buyer to pay for a diagnosis in a noisy, hype-heavy category. The website must therefore prove method, people, outcomes and intellectual honesty.
Real customer: name, logo and permission.
Real starting condition: the questions and baseline results.
Real finding: what the report discovered.
Real action: what the customer changed.
Real outcome: what changed afterwards, with caveats.
Transparent method: models, prompt design, dates, variance and limitations.
Founder credibility: why MentionBee is qualified to interpret the results.
A useful anti-hype promise: MentionBee does not promise that a schema tag or an llms.txt file will make ChatGPT recommend you. It shows what evidence exists, what is missing, and which interventions are worth testing first.
That stance is strategically valuable because much of the category overstates causality. Skepticism can become a brand asset.

18. Strategy v1.1 - What Changed
Revised product thesis: MentionBee should become the SME-focused AI Visibility Diagnosis-to-Execution System: FIND → EXPLAIN → FIX → VERIFY, with reliability and multilingual market context built into the measurement layer.
The deeper competitive scan shows that actionable recommendations, implementation-ready assets and full audit-to-fix loops already exist in the market. The conclusion is not that MentionBee must find a feature nobody else has. The practical opportunity is packaging and marketing: a low-friction one-off entry for SMEs, a useful diagnosis, concrete actions, honest methodology and strong proof - sold and distributed better than the category's many interchangeable audits.
The investor answer today: MentionBee does not yet have a single feature that can honestly be called unique. The opportunity is the combination and packaging: low-friction one-off entry for SMEs; buyer-intent and competitor diagnosis; execution-ready assets; explicit measurement uncertainty; multilingual testing; and re-measurement after implementation.

20. Measurement Reliability as Research & Brand Stance
AI visibility is probabilistic. Research in 2026 shows substantial repeated-sample variability and strong paraphrase brittleness. Single-run scores therefore risk false precision.
Research experiment: run a bounded Reliability Test on a small, publishable sample using natural paraphrases and repeated runs. The purpose is thought leadership and methodological honesty, not a report rewrite. Do not multiply API calls across every paid report unless evidence shows that the extra precision changes customer decisions or recommendations.
Possible category stance: “Your AI Visibility Score is probably too precise.” This is a marketing/research hypothesis, not yet a product promise. It must be demonstrated empirically before becoming a headline claim.
